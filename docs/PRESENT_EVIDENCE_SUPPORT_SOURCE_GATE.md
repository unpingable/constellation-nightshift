## Existing ownership and contract

Nightshift sends one sealed `nightshift.present_evidence_query.v1` to the
configured executable whose basename is exactly `pulse-support-resolver`. The
query binds the live cycle, nonce, observation, diagnostic-input set, subject,
scope, and exact delivered artifact identities. A response binds those same
coordinates, one authority identity, receiver-clock evaluation and expiry,
standing, and exact evidence or contradiction references.

The resolver port is read-only. It may consume, relate, and verify independent
support evidence. It must not:

- invoke NQ diagnostic acquisition;
- ask the diagnostic provider to run again;
- create the pulse/support occurrence used by its verdict;
- turn evidence replay into a new occurrence;
- infer present support from V3 origin or admission custody;
- emit AG, Docket, repair, restart, or other effect authority; or
- decide a stronger proposition than its qualified support source establishes.

Nightshift owns the final posture/currentness consequence. A support producer
does not emit `current = true`; a resolver does not own the resulting governed
reliance decision.

## Replay, staleness, and later support

The following identities remain distinct:

```text
replay(existing diagnostic acquisition)
!= deliberate successor diagnostic acquisition
!= replay(existing support occurrence)
!= produce a new support occurrence
!= resolve support applicability
```

For this exact semantic-identity family, a later independent support occurrence
may support the unchanged earlier diagnostic object. It establishes the same
bounded proposition at its own later occurrence; it does not refresh or alter
the diagnostic bytes or their evidence time. At receiver-clock expiry the
support becomes `expired` while both evidence objects remain historical. A
fresh independently acquired occurrence may restore only the support
component. This law does not generalize to another proposition.
